### Editorial Notes And Follow-Up

- New leaves: Evolutionary Computation & Optimization (GECCO, AutoML), Cognitive
  Science & Computational Cognition (CogSci), and ML Systems & Infrastructure
  (MLSys). They extend the existing seven families; each edition has at most
  four central leaves. LoG remains distinct from the existing LOD/AIS series.
- Annual cadence is explicit for these additions. ProbML's AABI archive records
  recent annual 2021-2026 editions. Estimated meeting dates are planning proxies,
  not evidence that the next venue or co-location has been announced.
- ICORE 2026 direct matches: AAMAS A, GECCO A, Interspeech A, CogSci B, ICIP B.
  CoRL is explicitly Unranked; no rank is inferred for the other five additions.
  Evidence uses the [official release export](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/?search=&by=all&source=ICORE2026&sort=atitle&page=1&do=Export)
  and portal IDs in data/icore_rankings.yml, not older CORE scores.
- CCF 2026 direct conference matches: AAMAS B (PDF page 58), CogSci B (71),
  GECCO C (59), ICIP C (49). AAMAS's unrelated journal entry must not be used.
  Evidence and the [public release page](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/By_category/2026-03-31/870181.shtml)
  are stored separately in data/ccf_rankings.yml.
- Historical acceptance evidence was found for AutoML 2024 (rounded 42%,
  Methods/ABCD), LoG 2023 (31.14%, proceedings full papers), and AAMAS 2026
  (chairs' reported approximately 25.5%, main full papers). Sources and scope
  are in data/acceptance_rates.yml. AAMAS's printed counts and reported rate do
  not exactly agree; preserve the reported approximate figure, not a fabricated
  reconciliation. Findings, extended abstracts, Blue Sky, and demos are excluded.
  Other additions remain Unknown until reliable submitted/accepted statistics
  are found; an accepted-paper list alone is not an acceptance denominator.
- Size labels are editorial estimates, not measured attendance, except that
  Interspeech's XL is grounded in its organizer description of 2,000+ attendees.
  Revisit these approximations when recent attendance figures are published.
- Priority checks: AutoML/ProbML 2027 announcements; CogSci 2027 CFP; GECCO's
  stale 2026 text on its 2027 CFP; ICIP/Interspeech exact main-paper hours;
  MLSys's homepage noon-PDT versus CFP 20:00-UTC discrepancy. Since ADR-040 the
  earlier noon-PDT instant (19:00 UTC) is stored with a cutoff_note citing the
  CFP's 20:00 UTC. Confirm Blue Sky's opening once its
  author-registration portal is actually available; Oct 20 has no exact hour.
- Consult project_docs/data_maintenance.md for the monthly evidence review.
  Update canonical YAML first, then the workbook and generated site/feeds.

## Using This Backlog

Follow [adding conferences](adding_conferences.md) for the complete multi-file
procedure; this backlog is only dated discovery and approval-status evidence.

Do not auto-import these candidates or their scraped deadlines. An addition
requires user approval, identity/alias checks, current official-source review,
up to four central subtopics, an explicitly sourced recurrence pattern, and
independent ranking/rate evidence. Preserve Unknown where evidence is absent.
Record the addition here, synchronize the canonical workbook mirror, and run
the validation, build, tests, and publication checks in development.md.
