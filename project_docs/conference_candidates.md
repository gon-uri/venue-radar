## Scope And Counts

The original comparison found **57 untracked series in total**, not 57 in
addition to the 11 initially selected venues. Those 11, a second batch of
15, and a third batch of 16 are now tracked, leaving **15 deferred candidates**. All 57 are preserved
below, split by their status. The 2026-10-31 related-venue additions also track
ICWSM, IUI and SLT from that deferred list, leaving 12 still deferred.
ICWM is a separate future-only candidate and is not part of that total.

The requested retrospective window was 2024-2026. The retrieved page contained
223 edition entries: 89 for 2025, 83 for 2026, 49 for 2027, and 2 for 2028, but
none for 2024. Its 172 retained 2025-2026 entries represent 80 series; 23 were
already tracked before the additions. This is an audit of the retained page,
not an exhaustive reconstruction of its unavailable 2024 history.

Years below are editions visible in the snapshot, including future listings
for series present in the retrospective window. Linked official sites are the
latest links supplied by AI Deadlines; they are discovery references, not a
verification of every candidate's schedule. Check current organizer sources
before adding a candidate. Topic groupings are editorial, not approved tags.
Normalized aliases include SIGKDD/KDD, FAccT/ACM FAccT, and ECML-PKDD/ECML PKDD.

### Editorial Notes And Follow-Up

- New leaves: Evolutionary Computation & Optimization (GECCO, AutoML), Cognitive
  Science & Computational Cognition (CogSci), and ML Systems & Infrastructure
  (MLSys). They extend the existing seven families; each edition has at most
  four central leaves. LoG remains distinct from the existing LOD/AIS series.
- Annual cadence is explicit for these additions. ProbML's AABI archive records
  recent annual 2021-2026 editions. Estimated meeting dates are planning proxies,
  not evidence that the next venue or co-location has been announced.
- ICORE 2026 direct matches: AAMAS A, GECCO A, Interspeech A, CogSci B, ICIP B.
  CoRL is explicitly Unranked; no rank is inferred for the other five additions.
  Evidence uses the [official release export](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/?search=&by=all&source=ICORE2026&sort=atitle&page=1&do=Export)
  and portal IDs in data/icore_rankings.yml, not older CORE scores.
- CCF 2026 direct conference matches: AAMAS B (PDF page 58), CogSci B (71),
  GECCO C (59), ICIP C (49). AAMAS's unrelated journal entry must not be used.
  Evidence and the [public release page](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/By_category/2026-03-31/870181.shtml)
  are stored separately in data/ccf_rankings.yml.
- Historical acceptance evidence was found for AutoML 2024 (rounded 42%,
  Methods/ABCD), LoG 2023 (31.14%, proceedings full papers), and AAMAS 2026
  (chairs' reported approximately 25.5%, main full papers). Sources and scope
  are in data/acceptance_rates.yml. AAMAS's printed counts and reported rate do
  not exactly agree; preserve the reported approximate figure, not a fabricated
  reconciliation. Findings, extended abstracts, Blue Sky, and demos are excluded.
  Other additions remain Unknown until reliable submitted/accepted statistics
  are found; an accepted-paper list alone is not an acceptance denominator.
- Size labels are editorial estimates, not measured attendance, except that
  Interspeech's XL is grounded in its organizer description of 2,000+ attendees.
  Revisit these approximations when recent attendance figures are published.
- Priority checks: AutoML/ProbML 2027 announcements; CogSci 2027 CFP; GECCO's
  stale 2026 text on its 2027 CFP; ICIP/Interspeech exact main-paper hours;
  MLSys's homepage noon-PDT versus CFP 20:00-UTC discrepancy. Since ADR-040 the
  earlier noon-PDT instant (19:00 UTC) is stored with a cutoff_note citing the
  CFP's 20:00 UTC. Confirm Blue Sky's opening once its
  author-registration portal is actually available; Oct 20 has no exact hour.
- Consult project_docs/data_maintenance.md for the monthly evidence review.
  Update canonical YAML first, then the workbook and generated site/feeds.

## Related-Venue Search (2026-10-30/31)

The owner asked for a comprehensive search for conferences related to the
catalog in topics, style and scientific level: non-predatory and well regarded
by their communities, even when small. Eight searchers (one per topic family)
proposed 201 venues; a separate reviewer checked each batch against official
and society pages and tried to reject it; a completeness critic looked for
gaps. 198 were vetted: 42 Recommended, 16 Strong consider (legitimate, often
top-ranked, only partly inside the catalog topics), 65 Consider, 71 Long shot
and 4 rejected. ICORE ranks were re-matched against the official ICORE2026
export on 2026-10-31. CCF, Google Scholar, size and acceptance figures are the
reviewers' notes and were not independently re-verified. The shared
web-search budget ran out partway through, so later facts rely on direct
fetches of official pages. Candidate facts are discovery evidence, not
calendar data: an addition still needs the full official-source review in
[adding conferences](adding_conferences.md).

The complete vetted data (every field, source URLs, search logs and status)
is in [candidate_search/related_venues_2026-10-31.json](candidate_search/related_venues_2026-10-31.json),
and [candidate_search/related_venues_2026-10-31.html](candidate_search/related_venues_2026-10-31.html)
is a filterable copy of the owner's review page that opens offline. The
owner's live review page (private to the owner) is
<https://claude.ai/artifact/QyCXxg2q1T9CyoSaRxPdqK>; its Add/Maybe/Skip marks
are kept there.

### Rejected After Vetting (4)

- APS GPS (APS Global Physics Summit (successor to the APS March Meeting), statistical/nonlinear and soft/living-matter sessions): Unquestionably legitimate, but it is a general-physics mega-meeting where the relevant part is a small slice. Abstracts are accepted if they follow the guidelines (no scientific selection), submission requires APS or reciprocal-society membership, and the 2027 window has already closed.
- APBC (Asia Pacific Bioinformatics Conference): Legitimate (ICORE B, CCF C, running since 2003), but bioinformatics and omics are outside the catalog's eight families, which cover clinical AI, imaging and biometrics. If a bioinformatics line were opened, ISMB or RECOMB should come first.
- SOUPS (Symposium on Usable Privacy and Security): Highly regarded (ICORE A, about 20% acceptance), but its core is the human factors of security and privacy. Little of its work is AI or ML, so it falls outside this catalog's scope.
- ERNSI (ERNSI Workshop on System Identification (European Research Network System Identification)): No open submission route. ERNSI 2026 registration was 'by invitation only', and contributions are collected through the network's local team leaders.
