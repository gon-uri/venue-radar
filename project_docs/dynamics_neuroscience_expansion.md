community venues. An abstract book, archived abstract or presentation is not
an archival full-paper proceedings publication. Publication eligibility can

## Topics And Evidence Gaps

Three focused leaves complement the unchanged eight-family scheme:

- Artificial Life & Adaptive Systems, under Complex Systems, Time Series & Signals: ALIFE and SAB.
- Neuromorphic & Brain-inspired Computing, under Neuroscience & Neurotechnology: NICE and IEEE BioCAS.
- Systems & Experimental Neuroscience, under Neuroscience & Neurotechnology: AREADNE, SfN and ICCN's extended profile.

Up to four primary tags remain visible. The additional-topic union and scope
prose remain curated and searchable through the existing system. A specialist
using ML does not automatically acquire a general-ML central identity.

The reviewed ICORE2026 export has no direct identities for these 16 series.
In particular, ALIFE is not IEEE International Symposium on Artificial Life
(portal 693), nor the removed ECAL identity. No rank is borrowed from either.
No new CCF rank or acceptance percentage is asserted without verified evidence;
these venues retain the existing Unranked/Unknown states. Abstract acceptance
rates must not be presented as full-paper selectivity.
