# Review: 2026-11-18 (Urgent Items, Next Editions And Decision Dates)

Reviewed: 2026-11-18. This is dated source evidence, not a replacement for the
[maintenance procedure](data_maintenance.md).

## Method

Ten research agents read official organizer pages: one for the urgent queue
(Act now, Recheck before deadline, and the Nov 15-17 extension rechecks), two
for the 15 rollover-ready series, one for the six missing successors and seven
old-year citations, and six for the 86 open main-paper editions with no
decision date. Five independent verifiers re-opened every cited page and tried
to refute each proposal. Of 115 proposals, 99 were accepted, 14 corrected and
2 rejected. Owner questions raised by the agents were settled as recorded
below; nothing is applied from an aggregator.

`last_checked` advances only for the 14 urgent-queue editions whose own pages
were read in full, plus ICANN 2027. Adding a decision date alone does not mark
a record as reviewed.

## Decision Dates

Decision dates now exist for 81 of the 86 open main-paper editions that lacked
one. 16 come from the edition's own official page (for example ICML May 3,
SIGIR Apr 5, Interspeech Jun 2, RSS Apr 30, ICMLA Jun 15). The other 65 are
estimated: the previous edition's official decision date, shifted by the
same number of days as the stored paper deadline. When the previous paper
deadline was extended, the projection uses the original (pre-extension)
deadline and decision pair, because the stored estimate was projected from the
original deadline (ADR-054).

Still without a decision date (the new review-queue section lists them):
CCN 2027 (no published 8-page paper decision
day), AMIA 2027 (site blocks readers; the archived call gives none), MLHC 2027
("Details coming soon"), SDM 2027 (SIAM pages refuse automated readers), ISWC
2027 (the 2026 dates page is gone) and the new CVPR 2028.

## Scope Profiles

The 51 series without a profile (AAAI, ACC, CHI, ECML PKDD, ICASSP, IJCAI,
MICCAI, PAKDD and others) now have one, so every series has an "About this
venue" summary. Four agents drafted them from official calls and scope pages;
three independent verifiers re-read every source, accepted 13 as drafted and
corrected 38: unsupported or promotional claims removed, dated themes
reworded, tags resting on a single list item dropped (ESANN, ICASSP, NER,
ICDM, BigData, MICCAI, SSCI and others), ECC's society named correctly
(EUCA) and ITISE's official topics page added. None was rejected. Profiles
read from an earlier call (CCS, ECAI, MLHC, ICONIP, SDM, MICCAI, ML4H and
others) stay queued until the newer call appears. ACPR's only official source
is served over http.

## Verification Limits

web.archive.org was unavailable to some agents' page reader; verifiers used
it where reachable. The ICRA, ACCV, MLSP and AMIA sites block automated
readers, so their evidence is an official-page snapshot or nothing. AALTD's and
ADMA's original 2026 decision dates come from the organizers' public site
repositories on GitHub.
